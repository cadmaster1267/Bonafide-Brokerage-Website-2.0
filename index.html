The Story of Bonafide Brokerage

Real estate has been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

My father built every home I lived in. My earliest years were in a home he built, and later, a magnificent home in the Hollywood Hills. It was there, that my lifelong love of real estate began. Seeing the vision, the planning, and the finished home planted a seed in me that would grow into a lifelong passion for real estate.

My career began in the beautiful mountain community of Lake Arrowhead, California, where I realized that real estate was more than just a job, it was something I was genuinely passionate about. It continued in Las Vegas, Nevada, collaborating with experienced brokers, learning, and building my knowledge of the industry. For years, I worked with families as they took important steps toward building their futures.

After earning my Real Estate Broker's License, Bonafide Brokerage was born. Bonafide Brokerage is built on a lifetime of mentors, and determination. But more importantly, it is built on knowledge and professional experience.

Whether you are Purchasing your very first home, Selling a home because life is changing, selling a family home filled with memories...Bonafide Brokerage is here to help guide you through the journey.

Our foundation is built on experience in real estate, and—most importantly—a genuine love for helping you start your next chapter. That is the dream we continue to build every day.
